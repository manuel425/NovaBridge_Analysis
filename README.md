# NovaBridge_Analysis
 I have developed Tableau worksheets to prevent energy waste and improve customer satisfaction in the utilities sector.


 KPIs
-	Calculate the total number of Spikes.
-	Calculate the total number of resolved cases.
-	Calculate the total number of alerts.
-	Calculate the energy usage.
-	Compare the energy usage and baseline usage.
-	Determine the daily energy usage grouping by housing type.
-	Calculate the regional energy distribution.
-	Classify anomalies in household.
-	Compare the energy to the percentage.
-	Determine the spike intensity over a period.
-	Determine the spike level in every region.
-	Compare the spike level during the weekend and weekday.
-	Show an overview of the energy consumption.
-	Show an overview of the spike.

- Dashboard Interaction <a href='https://github.com/manuel425/NovaBridge_Analysis/blob/main/Energy%20Overview.png'> View Dashboard</a>

 Process
- Imported raw meter readings and calculated baseline using google sheets.
- Flag readings above 40% and log it into structured a sheet.
- Created Tableau dashboards for real-time trends and regional distribution
- Present the actionable insights to relevant stakeholders
